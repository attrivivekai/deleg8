# About deleg8

**Mission:** give every engineering team an AI junior employee that works the backlog through the same tools humans use:
tickets, repos, CI and code review.

**Tagline:** "AI employees, delegated."

## Brand voice (applies to all website copy)
- Plain, confident, specific. Say what the product does, not how revolutionary it is.
- **No exclamation marks** in marketing copy. (Emoji are OK on the thanks page only.)
- Say "AI junior" or "AI employee", never "bot" or "robot".
- Use sentence case for headings ("How it works", not "How It Works").
- Our colors: brand purple `#7c5cff`, accent mint `#22d3a6`.
